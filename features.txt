AC8-VR_MankTank_v0.3.2 features

Cockpit HUD layout
- Resolution independence - layouts should still work and hud elements
  should stay in place regardless of your current resolution.
- Move and resize the radar, center ladder, aircraft status, mission
  information and wingman status live from UEVR's Script UI.
- Move score, current target and time remaining together as Mission
  Information, then fine-tune each row separately.
- Move wingman orders, the comms portrait and radio messages independently.
- Keep wingman status above the radar as it changes between normal and
  expanded modes. Give the status group its own position and scale.
- Move and scale the pitch ladder, speed, altitude and flight path marker
  together. Trim the flight path marker separately while retaining its
  in-flight movement.
- Start with a curved VR HUD and adjust its distance, overall size and
  curvature in UEVR.

Aircraft presets and cockpit camera
- Save a HUD layout, UEVR UI Size, and cockpit camera offsets for each
  aircraft. Save the HUD and camera separately or with one button.
- Adjust your seated position forward, back, sideways and vertically.
  Adjust perceived cockpit size while in cockpit view.
- Set global HUD and camera defaults for aircraft without their own presets,
  or use a shared HUD layout across all aircraft.
- Reload the selected saved layout or turn per-plane settings off temporarily.
  Your saved presets stay available when you turn them back on.
- Reduce game-driven camera shakes and camera animations during flight
  with the optional Plane Camera module.

Glow and readability
- Recreate the game's HUD glow in VR, composited into each eye's view.
- Apply glow selectively so supported radio text, tutorial cards and menu
  panels stay readable. Keep radar and comms portrait treatment separate
  from the surrounding HUD.
- Respect the dark backing of pause and retry menus while retaining
  the flight HUD behind them.
- Keep the game's native tutorial card fill, remove the extra background
  rectangle and suppress tutorial blur.

Targets and weapon displays
- Resize world-target boxes and labels for VR, with adjusted text spacing
  and placement. Retain the game's target introduction animation.
- Resize assignment and escort markers. Keep the aerial refueling guide's
  artwork and labels aligned as a group.
- Reduce and reposition off-screen target indicators for more room near
  the edges. Keep the forward locator and off-screen indicators aligned
  to their own centers.
- Resize supported target-lock seeker artwork. Adjust the SAAM circle size
  from the HUD controls.
- Align the bomb trajectory display with the flight path marker. Fine-tune
  the bomb projection and landing marker height in Script UI while the
  game continues to calculate the actual impact point.

Cinematics, hangar and transitions
- Switch to the game's authored camera framing for cinematics and the
  hangar, including Aircraft Set. Return to your flight settings afterward.
- Use mission loading coverage and in-game fades to cover the handoff
  between menus and flight. Center the loading indicator for VR.
- Move through pause and retry choices one step per keyboard or controller
  press. Keep flight warnings and messages responsive during HUD updates.

Modular profile and compatibility
- Keep AC8_VR-Core.lua enabled. The seven other scripts let you choose
  whether to use aircraft presets, cinematics and hangar presentation,
  HUD glow, HUD layout, loading, camera stabilization and target changes.
- This build reduces repeated HUD lookups and rendering work and uses
  no development capture dumps, performance reports or mission-exit
  discovery statistics. The mod keeps the glow status and recovery data
  it uses in play, along with error messages.
- Requires UEVR with OpenXR and Native Stereo. Restart the game and
  inject again after changing the game resolution or UEVR render resolution.
- For module toggles, cockpit save steps and troubleshooting, see README.md
  and FAQ.md.
